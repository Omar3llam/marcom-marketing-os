Build Your Marketing Foundation in 30 Seconds

Lumi's Context Engine is the foundation of everything. It locks your brand's full marketing intelligence so every subsequent output is hyper-specific to your business — never generic.

1Drop your URL — Lumi scrapes and analyses your site automatically, extracting positioning, audience, and competitor signals.
2Confirm or correct — Lumi shows you what it found with confidence levels. You fill any gaps.
3Context locked — Your profile is stored and powers every tool. One setup, forever contextual.
18 Marketing Tools, One Conversation

From GTM strategy to email sequences, Lumi's tool suite covers every marketing need. Each tool is context-aware — outputs are tailored to your locked business profile, never generic templates.

1State your goal — "Build me a lead gen framework" or "Analyse my top 3 competitors."
2Lumi auto-routes to the right tool from the 18-tool suite, no switching required.
3Get a structured output — exportable to CRM, downloadable as CSV, or shareable via client link.
A Full Month of Content. Done in Minutes.

Lumi generates a complete 4-week social media calendar with platform-specific briefs, content pillars, hooks, CTAs, and funnel stage mapping — all tailored to your market and brand voice.

1Specify platforms — LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, or all of them.
2Lumi builds 28 posts with hooks, formats, funnel stages, and cultural moment alignment.
3Export to CSV or sync directly to your CRM workflow.
10-Step Content Audit. Brutal. Honest. Constructive.

Submit any post, ad, email, or caption. Lumi runs it through a 10-step interrogation — from AI probability detection to hook analysis and audience conviction — and rewrites anything that fails.

1Submit your content — text, image, or both. Specify platform, funnel stage, format, and audience.
2Get a full scorecard — AI probability, hook score, commercial impact, authority signal, and more.
3Receive the rewrite — if the score warrants it, Lumi rewrites automatically and explains every change.
Scale, Hold, Kill. Data-Driven Media Buying.

Paste your campaign metrics and Lumi delivers 2025/2026-calibrated decisions for every ad set — with exact reasoning, MER/CAC flag analysis, and horizontal scaling recommendations.

1Paste your metrics — Meta ASC, Google Ads, TikTok, or any ad platform data.
2Lumi analyses each campaign — compares against your KPIs and flags MER/CAC violations.
3Get ranked decisions — Scale, Hold, Optimize, Pause, or Kill, sorted by impact score.
